# Rotaract District 3011 — District DAC Secretariat Handbook & Platform Guide

**Target Audience:** District Council (DAC), District Secretariat, Zonal Representatives, and Club Officers  
**Rotary Year:** 2026–2027  
**District:** Rotaract District Organization 3011 (Delhi NCR)  
**Document Type:** Official Feature Guide & Operations Backsheet

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Platform Purpose

The Rotaract District 3011 digital platform is the official unified operational system for all 90+ chartered clubs across Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Noida, and adjoining zones. It integrates public engagement, club administration, monthly reporting, performance scoring, member directories, and district governance into a seamless, high-speed digital experience.

### Module Breakdown:
1. **Public District Website (`rotaract3011.org`):**
   * **Visual Hero & Roadmap:** Milestone progression tracking district goals and achievements across all 4 pillars.
   * **Interactive Club Map:** Geographic cluster map allowing potential members, donors, and Rotary partners to discover clubs by zone and institution.
   * **Public Showcase:** High-impact community stories, health camps, vocational workshops, and environmental drives published by clubs.
   * **Heritage Gallery:** Complete archival history of Past District Rotaract Representatives (PDRRs) with historical tenures and citations.
   * **Live Calendar:** Synchronized calendar of district conferences, installations, conclaves, and zonal sports meets.

2. **District Secretariat Portal (`/portal`):**
   * **Monthly Reporting Engine:** Streamlined reporting workflow for club secretaries with autosave, activity breakdown, and validation.
   * **Secretariat Query Thread:** Two-way audit thread allowing the District Reporting Committee to ask questions and request clarifications before awarding points.
   * **Assisted Scoring & Formula Points:** Automated calculation of on-time filing, membership growth, dual-membership, and avenue participation.
   * **Member Management & Password Recovery:** Club presidents and district admins can view club rosters, approve registrations, generate temporary reset passwords, or set custom login credentials.
   * **Professional Skill Directory:** Cross-district directory where members connect based on industry skills (Tech, Medical, Legal, Design, Finance).

## 3. District Roles & Administrative Hierarchy

| Role | Operational Scope | Key Responsibilities & Powers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Super Admin / DRR** | Full District | Overall district oversight, policy enforcement, role assignment, emergency unlocks, and final score approvals. |
| **District General Secretary / Secretariat** | District Governance | Reviews monthly reports, manages query threads, verifies event attendances, scores club activities, and updates calendar. |
| **Zonal Representatives (ZR / ZS)** | Assigned Zone | Mentors assigned clubs within Zone Prithvi, Agni, Vayu, or Akash; tracks timely report submission and zonal attendance. |
| **Club President & Secretary** | Home Club | Manages club member approvals, resets member passwords, submits monthly reports, registers club events, and pushes stories to showcase. |
| **General Rotaractor** | Personal Profile | Attends district events, downloads digital membership card, accesses member directory, and updates professional skills. |

## 4. End-to-End Monthly Reporting & Showcase Workflow

```
┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│ 1. Club Drafts  │       │ 2. Club Submits │       │ 3. Secretariat  │       │ 4. Verified     │
│   Report Online ├──────►│   Before 5th    ├──────►│   Review & Query├──────►│   Points Scored │
└─────────────────┘       └────────┬────────┘       └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
                                   │
                                   ▼
                          ┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
                          │ Push Project to ├──────►│ Showcase Post   │
                          │ Showcase Modal  │       │ Published Live  │
                          └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
```

1. **Drafting & Submissions:** By the 5th of each month, club officers log in and record club meetings, project details, beneficiaries, and photographs.
2. **Instant Showcase Conversion:** From the report detail screen, club officers can click **"Push to Showcase"** on any reported project. The platform automatically fills project details, asks for high-res photos, and sends the story directly to the District Public Relations team for instant publication.
3. **Secretariat Review & Scoring:** The district team reviews each avenue, triggers queries if documentation is missing, and awards points that immediately reflect on the District Leaderboard.

## 5. Upcoming Flagship Initiatives (RY 2026–27)

### 1. Rotaract Car Pooling Initiative
* **Objective:** Enable eco-friendly, cost-effective travel and inter-club bonding for delegates attending district events, installations, and mega projects.
* **Functionality:** Event attendees can list available vehicle seats or request rides based on departure zones (South Delhi, West Delhi, Gurugram, Noida). Verified member badge authentication ensures total safety.

### 2. RIDE Subdomain Website (`ride.rotaract3011.org`)
* **Objective:** Rotaract International District Exchange (RIDE) portal coordinating domestic and international youth exchange programs.
* **Functionality:** Incoming foreign delegations can view host club allocations, cultural itineraries, homestay matching, and emergency contact details, while District 3011 members can apply for outgoing international exchange fellowships.
